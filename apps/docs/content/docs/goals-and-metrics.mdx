A **goal** is a statement of what the company, a floor, a squad, or an initiative is working toward in a period. A goal holds **key results**, and each key result follows one **metric**. Progress and confidence are calculated from the metrics, so the goal shows where it stands without anyone writing a status report.

## Metrics

A metric is a named series with a unit and a direction. Each reading has a value, a time, and a scope: the company, a floor, a squad, or an initiative. Metrics come from three sources.

**Built-in** metrics are calculated nightly from the workspace's own records:

| Area | Metrics |
| --- | --- |
| Delivery | `delivery.issues_done`, `delivery.cycle_time_days`, `delivery.rework_rate` |
| Runs | `runs.count`, `runs.success_rate`, `runs.cost_usd` |
| Proposals | `proposals.acceptance_rate`, `proposals.decision_hours` |
| Routines | `routines.compliance_rate` |
| Pipeline | `pipeline.initiatives_active`, `pipeline.stalled`, `pipeline.stage_days` |
| Knowledge | `knowledge.docs_published`, `knowledge.fresh_ratio`, `knowledge.retrieval_hit_rate` |
| Brief | `brief.usefulness` |

`knowledge.*` metrics and `brief.usefulness` are company-scope only. Run cost counts only what the provider reports.

**Manual** readings come from a member or an agent:

```bash
multica metric list
multica metric report --key runs.count --scope squad:<squad-id> --value 42 --note "Checked by hand"
multica metric report --key mrr --scope initiative:<initiative-id> --value 1200 --observed-at 2026-11-01T09:00:00Z
```

`--scope` takes `company` (the default), `floor:<id>`, `squad:<id>`, or `initiative:<id>`. Built-in metrics refuse manual readings.

**Webhook** readings bring figures from outside the product, such as signups, revenue, or uptime. Post them with a personal access token of a workspace member (see [Auth tokens](/auth-tokens)):

```bash
curl -X POST "$MULTICA_API/api/metrics/signups/readings" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $MULTICA_TOKEN" -H "X-Workspace-ID: $WORKSPACE_ID" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"value": 128, "scope_type": "company", "source": "webhook", "note": "Weekly export"}'
```

The body takes `value`, `scope_type`, `scope_id` (for non-company scopes), `observed_at`, `note`, and `source`. The endpoint is rate-limited to 60 reports per minute for each workspace and metric. A metric created by a report takes its name, unit, and direction from the body. Reports larger than 16 KiB are refused.

Readings are kept in full for 90 days. Older readings are reduced to one value per day.

## Goals and key results

A key result compares a metric's latest reading with a start value and a target:

- Its progress is the share of the way from the start to the target, clamped between 0 and 1. Direction is handled by the sign of the difference.
- A goal's progress is the weighted mean of its key results, with each weight from 1 to 10.
- Confidence compares progress with a straight line from the period's start to its end. It is `on_track` when progress is at least the expected share minus ten points, `at_risk` down to thirty points below, and `off_track` beyond that. With no reading yet it is `unknown`.

The owner can override the confidence with a note. The override shows as one.

A goal has a status: `draft`, `active`, `achieved`, `missed`, or `dropped`. Activating a goal requires at least one key result. The server refuses a goal without one.

**Who sets goals.** You and admins create company and floor goals, in the **Goals** tab. A squad's goals come from a planning cycle. At the start of each period, the platform opens a planning issue for each squad with a charter. It contains the company goals and the squad's charter. The leader answers with a `goal` proposal, and approving it activates the goal. See [Proposals](/proposals).

The leader's briefing lists the squad's active key results as **Your goals this period**. The squad's operating review reports progress against them.

<Callout type="info">
The Goals tab in **Governance** shows the goals as a tree: company, floors, squads, and initiatives. Each key result has a sparkline. The brief's **Goals** section names the goals that changed state, and each at-risk or off-track goal with the metric that moved.
</Callout>

## Limits

- The goal and metric endpoints are `GET /api/goals`, `POST /api/goals`, `GET`, `PATCH`, and `DELETE /api/goals/{id}`, `POST /api/goals/{id}/activate`, `POST /api/goals/{id}/status`, `PUT /api/goals/{id}/confidence`, `GET /api/metrics`, and `GET` and `POST /api/metrics/{key}/readings`. Goals have no CLI command.
- Company import does not carry squad-scoped or initiative-scoped goals. See [Company as code](/company-as-code).
