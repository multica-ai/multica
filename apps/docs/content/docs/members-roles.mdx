In a [workspace](/workspaces), `owner`, `admin`, and `member` can create issues and post comments. Their permissions differ when managing settings and people. A [guest](#guests) can read and comment on issues only in projects shared with them.

## Permissions for owners, admins, and members [#the-three-roles]

| Action | owner | admin | member |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| View the workspace and collaborate day to day | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Change workspace settings | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| Invite an `admin` or `member` | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| Change or remove an `admin` or `member` | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| Grant, revoke, or remove `owner` | ✓ | — | — |
| Delete the workspace | ✓ | — | — |

A workspace must always keep at least one `owner`: the last `owner` cannot be demoted, removed, or leave.

<RolesDemo />

<Callout type="info">
Guests cannot run agents. Each agent has its own Access scope, and `owner` and `admin` cannot bypass it to run agents they were not granted. See [Agents](/agents#permissions-and-access).
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
Roles do not open private projects either. An `owner` or `admin` sees a private project only when they are on its member list. See [Private projects](/projects#private-projects).
</Callout>

## Invite members

`owner` and `admin` open **Settings → Members**, click **Invite member**, enter an email, pick a role, and send. Invitations can grant `admin`, `member`, or [guest](#guests) access; an `owner` must join the workspace first and then be promoted by an existing `owner`.

![Member management: the invite form and the roster with roles](/images/docs/members-roster.webp)

The invitee accepts by logging in with that email — no prior signup is needed. Invitations are valid for 7 days; resend after they expire. On self-hosted instances that restrict signup, a live pending invitation allows that email to create an account without a separate allowlist entry — see [Auth setup](/auth-setup#invitations-and-signup-restrictions).

The invitation link appears only in the email; there is no place in the product to view or copy it. If the email fails to send, the invitation record still exists — revoke it and send a new one.

When you need a link you can pass along yourself, generate one under **Share links** in **Settings → Members** by choosing a role and an expiry. Anyone with the link can join with that role until it expires or is revoked, as long as the workspace has a free member slot (see below).

### Member limit

On Multica Cloud, a workspace on the Free plan can have up to 5 members. Members and pending invitations each take a slot; guests take none. While the limit applies, a new invitation, an accepted invitation or a share-link join that would go past it is refused until a slot frees up (an invitation is revoked or a member removed) or the workspace upgrades. Existing members are never removed. When the workspace is full, admins see **Upgrade and invite** in the invite dialog: once the payment goes through, **Settings → Billing** offers to send the held invitation. On Pro and Business each member takes a paid seat. Self-hosted workspaces have no member limit.

## Guests

A guest sees only the projects shared with it. In those projects it can read issues, comment, react, and follow them; it cannot create or edit issues, run or mention agents, or open workspace settings, and it sees no other projects, agents, autopilots, or runtimes. Guests are free: they take no paid seat and no member slot.

`owner` and `admin` invite a guest by choosing **Guest** in the invite dialog and picking the projects it can see. From a member's actions menu they can make a member a guest, which frees its seat and takes back its agents, autopilots, and runtime access; make a guest a member, which takes a seat; or change a guest's projects with **Manage accessible projects**. Like everything else about a private project, a guest's access to one is shown and changed only by people on that project's member list; changing a guest's projects leaves its access to private projects you are not on as it is.

On Multica Cloud, guests are included in Pro and Business. When a trial ends or a workspace moves to Free, existing guests keep their access and can still be removed, narrowed, or made members, but no new guests can be added. Self-hosted workspaces have guests.

## Change roles

In the member list, open the actions menu at the right of a member's row and choose **Change role**. The new role takes effect immediately.

An `admin` can move roles between `admin` and `member`, and can remove members holding those two roles. Any change involving `owner` — promoting, changing, or removing — can only be performed by an `owner`.

## Remove a member

A removed member loses workspace access immediately. Issues, comments, and other collaboration records they created stay in the workspace.

<Callout type="warning">
Removing a member also deactivates the runtimes they own, archives the agents bound to them, and cancels runs that have not finished. Grants the member received in agent Access scopes are cleared as well and are not restored automatically if they rejoin.
</Callout>
