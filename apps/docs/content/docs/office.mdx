The office draws your workspace as a company. Squads sit in rooms, rooms sit on floors, and agents sit at desks in their home squad's room. Each agent shows what it is doing, and anything waiting for your decision is marked.

<Callout type="info">
The office runs in the web app and the desktop app. The mobile app has no office.
</Callout>

## Floors

A floor is a named group of six squad rooms, arranged three across and two down. Floors appear as tabs at the top of the office.

- Floor names are yours. A squad from a template lands on the template's floor, so the **Research** template creates a `Research` floor. Other floors are named `Floor 1`, `Floor 2`, and so on.
- Owners and admins add a floor with the `+` at the end of the tab strip, rename it by double-clicking its tab, and use the tab menu to move it or delete it.
- A floor can be deleted only when no squad is seated on it. Move or unseat its squads first.
- A workspace can have up to 16 floors.

## Squads on the floor

Each squad holds at most one seat, a floor and a room slot. An unseated squad is not drawn.

- A new squad takes the lowest free room on the floor it belongs to. When every floor is full, the office creates the next `Floor N`.
- Each room has ten desks. The squad's leader sits first, then agents in the order they joined. A squad with more than ten agents shows a chip such as **2 more agents**, which opens the squad panel.
- Human members are not drawn. Empty desks stay empty, so a small squad does not rearrange the room.
- Clicking a room's name opens the squad panel: the squad's description, its members and their state, its autopilots, and its proposals waiting for a decision.
- An empty room offers **Create squad**.
- A room that another squad holds cannot be taken. Move the other squad first.

## Home squads

An agent is drawn at a desk of one squad only, its **home squad**. An agent can belong to several squads; it is drawn only in its home one.

- The first squad an agent joins becomes its home. The leader's first squad becomes the leader's home.
- Change the home squad from the agent panel, under **Home squad**. Clearing it leaves the agent unassigned, and it is not drawn anywhere.
- Removing an agent from its home squad, or archiving that squad, moves the agent to another live squad it still belongs to. If it has none, the agent becomes unassigned.

## The unseated tray

Squads without a room and agents without a home squad are listed in a strip labelled **Not on a floor**. Each chip has a menu:

- For a squad, **seat in a room**: pick a floor and a free room.
- For an agent, **set home squad**.

When any squad has no room, owners and admins see a banner with **Seat all**. It places every unseated squad, in order, on the next free rooms. Nothing changes until you press it. If the floor limit stops it, the squads that did not fit stay in the tray and the message says how many.

## What each agent shows

Each agent has a tag over its desk, chosen in this order:

1. **Needs you** when it leads a squad with a proposal waiting for a decision.
2. The status of the issue it is working on. An issue assigned to a squad counts for that squad's leader.
3. **Waiting for work** when it has no open issue.

## Decisions and proposals

A proposal is a squad's request for a decision from you. It contains a summary, at least one evidence link, a recommendation, the options considered, cost and risk, and the decision being asked for. Each proposal is backed by an issue, so comments and history work as they do on any issue.

Proposals do not notify you on their own. They wait in the Decisions queue, and the Secretary's daily brief lists them. The exception is an urgent proposal, which the squad has to justify. An urgent proposal is assigned to the workspace owner at once.

To review them, open **Decisions** from the office header, or from the count on a room badge. Each proposal shows the rendered body, the proposing squad, and any squad it would hand work to.

- **Approve** marks the proposal approved. If it names a handoff squad, the office creates a project named after the proposal, with a kickoff issue assigned to that squad. The squad's leader is woken on the new issue.
- **Reject** closes the proposal and posts your note as a comment that mentions the proposing squad's leader, so the squad can revise and file again.
- The note is optional on both actions.

Only owners and admins can decide. Agents cannot, including the Secretary.

<Callout type="info">
Agents file proposals with `multica proposal create`. Each squad is limited to three pending proposals, three created in any rolling seven days, and one urgent proposal in any rolling seven days. The workspace holds at most ten pending proposals. When a limit applies, the command says which one, and the squad must decide its pending proposals before filing more.
</Callout>
