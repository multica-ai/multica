# P7 — Wrap pattern proof (comment-card.tsx)

**Verdict:** NO-GO for this file. Comment-card must stay L3 (CEREBRO-PATCH inline).

**Hypothesis:** L2 wrapper pattern works end-to-end for at least one component (comment-card).

## What we found

- **File size:** 544 lines (cerebro) vs ~616 lines (upstream/main).
- **Diff vs upstream/main:** 68 insertions, 140 deletions, 208 lines touched.
- **Cerebro-specific changes (4 distinct, scattered concerns):**
  1. **Removed** upstream's `canModerate` admin-moderation prop + branching — 4 sites
  2. **Removed** upstream's `memo(CommentCardImpl)` perf wrapper
  3. **Removed** upstream's `enableHoverCard showStatusDot` from `ActorAvatar` — 2 sites
  4. **Added** `flattenReplies` helper sorting by `created_at` (JEH-345 fix)
  5. **Added** mobile padding tweaks — 8 className sites
  6. **Added** `attachments={entry.attachments}` to `ReadonlyContent` — 2 sites
  7. **Extracted** `AttachmentList` to its own file (cerebro-only)

## Why wrap fails

- **Component prop shape does NOT allow composition.** `CommentCard` renders a monolithic `Card` with internal `CommentRow` children, internal dropdown menus, internal collapsible. No slots, no render props, no className overrides.
- **Removals, not additions** — most cerebro work strips upstream features. A wrapper composes; it cannot un-render an internal child.
- **Estimated wrapper size:** ~450+ lines (essentially full re-implementation of `CommentRow` + `CommentCard` JSX). Far exceeds 150-line escalation threshold.

## Strategic implication

P7 was meant to validate that wrap could absorb at least one comment-related file. It cannot — but this is **expected behavior** under the plan's escalation rule: "Hvis en wrapper koster >150 linjer eller dublerer >30% af upstream-komponentens logik → drop wrapping for den fil og flyt til L3."

The wrap pattern itself remains technically feasible (P2 confirms path-alias/barrel-shadow works); we just need to pick the right files. comment-card belongs in the L3 bucket from the start.

## Recommendation

**Keep comment-card as L3 inline patch** with `CEREBRO-PATCH` markers at 6 concerns:

- 4 sites: `CEREBRO-PATCH(remove-canModerate): admin-moderation removed`
- 1 site: `CEREBRO-PATCH(jeh-345-reply-order): chronological replies via flattenReplies`
- 8 sites: `CEREBRO-PATCH(mobile-padding): mobile width tweaks` (consider consolidating to 1 marker per logical group)
- 2 sites: `CEREBRO-PATCH(attachments): pass entry.attachments to ReadonlyContent`

**Phase 3 list update:** drop comment-card from the 14-wrapper plan → 13 wrappers. Add to Phase 4 marker list.

**Re-evaluation trigger:** after wrapper #5 in Phase 3, gather wrap-economics. If 3+ wrappers escalate to L3 → re-assess remaining 9 (existing escalation rule).
